# Company Demo: NEXORA Industries

## Overview
NEXORA Industries is a fictional mid-sized B2B SaaS company founded in 2019, headquartered in Austin, Texas. They provide AI-powered supply chain optimization platforms for manufacturing enterprises. The company recently closed a Series B funding round ($45M) and is repositioning from a "startup" to a "scale-up" identity.

## Brand Identity
- **Name**: NEXORA Industries
- **Tagline**: "Intelligence That Moves the World"
- **Personality**: Confident, precise, futuristic-but-grounded, engineering-first
- **Core Values**: Transparency, Relentless Optimization, Human-Centered Automation

## Products & Services
1. **Nexora Flow** — Real-time supply chain visibility dashboard with predictive analytics
2. **Nexora Synapse** — ML-powered demand forecasting engine
3. **Nexora Link** — API-first integration layer for ERP systems (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite)

## Target Audience
- Primary: VP of Operations, Supply Chain Directors at mid-to-large manufacturers ($100M+ revenue)
- Secondary: CTOs and IT Directors evaluating enterprise SaaS vendors
- Decision drivers: ROI clarity, implementation speed, security compliance (SOC 2, ISO 27001)

## Key Messaging
- "Reduce inventory waste by up to 34% within 90 days"
- "Trusted by 3 of the top 10 automotive parts manufacturers in North America"
- "Deploy in weeks, not years"

## Website Requirements
The landing page must include:
1. **Hero Section**: Bold statement with a demo CTA and social proof (client logos)
2. **Problem/Solution**: The "before NEXORA" vs "after NEXORA" narrative
3. **Product Cards**: The three products with distinct visual identity but unified aesthetic
4. **Case Study Snippet**: A metrics-driven highlight (e.g., "How Acme Manufacturing cut lead times by 28%")
5. **Trust/Compliance**: SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR badges
6. **CTA Section**: Two paths — "Schedule a Demo" (primary) and "Talk to Sales" (secondary)
7. **Footer**: Clean, structured with links to Docs, Careers, Privacy, and Status page

## Design Direction Notes
- The aesthetic should feel like a premium enterprise tool, not a consumer app.
- Think: dense information architecture that still breathes, monospace for data points, sharp geometry.
- Avoid: playful illustrations, pastel colors, excessive whitespace that feels "empty" rather than "confident".
- Preferred paradigm: High-Fidelity Minimalism with subtle Soft Brutalist accents (sharp borders, bold typographic hierarchy).
