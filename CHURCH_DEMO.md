# Church Demo: Resurrection Light Community Church (RLCC)

## Overview
Resurrection Light Community Church is a fictional multicultural, non-denominational Christian congregation located in Portland, Oregon. Founded in 2008, the church has grown from a 30-person home gathering to a 400+ member community with two weekly services. They are known for their emphasis on practical faith, community service, and contemporary worship.

## Identity & Culture
- **Name**: Resurrection Light Community Church (RLCC)
- **Tagline**: "Where Faith Meets Life"
- **Personality**: Warm, inclusive, modern-but-reverent, community-focused
- **Core Values**: Authentic Worship, Radical Hospitality, Continuous Growth, Local Impact

## Ministries & Programs
1. **RLCC Kids** — Sunday programs for infants through 5th grade
2. **The Forge Youth** — Middle and high school ministry (Wednesdays)
3. **Life Groups** — Small group gatherings in homes throughout the week
4. **Serve PDX** — Monthly community outreach (food banks, shelter support, neighborhood cleanups)
5. **The Well** — Midweek prayer and worship night (Tuesdays)

## Service Times
- **Sunday**: 9:00 AM (Contemporary) & 11:00 AM (Blended)
- **Tuesday**: 7:00 PM — The Well (prayer & worship)
- **Wednesday**: 6:30 PM — The Forge Youth

## Target Audience
- Primary: Young families (30s-40s), young professionals, and college students in Portland metro
- Secondary: Individuals seeking a church home after deconstruction/reconstruction of faith
- Decision drivers: Authentic community, meaningful teaching, opportunities to serve locally

## Key Messaging
- "No perfect people allowed. Come as you are."
- "Faith isn't just Sunday morning — it's how we live Monday through Saturday."
- "A church that looks like the city it serves."

## Website Requirements
The landing page must include:
1. **Hero Section**: Warm, inviting welcome with service times and a "Plan Your Visit" CTA
2. **About Snippet**: Brief mission statement with a link to the full story
3. **Ministries Grid**: Visual cards for Kids, Youth, Life Groups, Serve PDX, The Well
4. **Upcoming Events**: Highlight the next Serve PDX event and a sermon series
5. **Sermon/Teaching**: Embed or link to the latest message ("The Art of Neighboring" series)
6. **Contact/Location**: Address, parking info, and a "Contact Us" form
7. **Footer**: Links to Give, Serve, Events, Statement of Faith, and social media

## Design Direction Notes
- The aesthetic should feel welcoming and structured without being corporate.
- Think: warm earth tones with a single vibrant accent, organic shapes balanced with clean grid systems, readable typography for all ages.
- Avoid: overly trendy "startup" aesthetics, neon colors, or sterile minimalist coldness.
- Preferred paradigm: Bento Box spatial grid with warm, tactile elements and generous whitespace that feels open rather than empty.
